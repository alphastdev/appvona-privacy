# Appvona Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 15, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Appvona is a business management and appointment booking platform operated by **Lagombytes** ("Appvona", "we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose information when you access or use Appvona's websites, business management tools, appointment booking services, messaging integrations, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Appvona is currently under development and testing. Some features described in this Policy may become available as the Services are developed and released. We will update this Policy when our data practices change.

## 2. Our Role in Processing Personal Data

Appvona serves both businesses and their customers.

- **Business account information:** When a business owner or employee creates or uses an Appvona account, Appvona generally processes their account information to provide and secure the Services.
- **Customer appointment information:** Businesses using Appvona may enter or receive personal information about their customers, such as names, phone numbers, appointment details, and service preferences. In these cases, the business generally determines why and how that customer information is used, while Appvona processes it on the business's behalf to provide the Services.

If you are a customer who booked an appointment with a business using Appvona, you may also contact that business directly about how it uses your information.

## 3. Information We May Collect

Depending on which Services are used, we may collect the following categories of information:

### 3.1 Business and Account Information

- Name and contact details of business owners and employees.
- Email address and phone number.
- Business name, business category, and business contact details.
- Branch information, business hours, and service details.
- Account credentials and authentication-related information.
- Account settings, permissions, and preferences.

### 3.2 Appointment and Customer Information

When businesses use Appvona to manage appointments, the information processed may include:

- Customer name and phone number.
- Appointment date, time, and status.
- Selected service, branch, and employee.
- Appointment notes or other information entered by the business or customer.
- Appointment history and related communications.

Businesses are responsible for ensuring that they have an appropriate legal basis to provide customer information to Appvona.

### 3.3 Messaging and Integration Information

If a business connects a messaging service such as WhatsApp or Telegram, we may process information necessary to operate that integration, including:

- Business messaging account and phone number identifiers.
- Customer messaging identifiers and phone numbers.
- Messages sent to or received through the integration.
- Message delivery and status information.
- Integration configuration, authorization tokens, and connection status.

WhatsApp services are provided through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. When WhatsApp is connected, information may be exchanged with Meta in accordance with Meta's applicable terms and privacy policies.

### 3.4 Technical and Usage Information

We may collect technical information needed to operate, maintain, and secure the Services, such as:

- IP address.
- Browser and device information.
- Log records and timestamps.
- Authentication and security events.
- Service usage and diagnostic information.

## 4. How We Use Information

We may use information for the following purposes:

- Creating and managing business accounts.
- Providing appointment booking and management services.
- Managing businesses, branches, employees, and services.
- Checking appointment availability and preventing scheduling conflicts.
- Sending appointment confirmations, reminders, and service-related messages.
- Operating WhatsApp, Telegram, and other authorized integrations.
- Maintaining account security and preventing misuse.
- Providing customer support.
- Troubleshooting, maintaining, and improving the Services.
- Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not use personal information for purposes incompatible with this Policy.

## 5. WhatsApp and Third-Party Integrations

Businesses may choose to connect their own WhatsApp Business accounts to Appvona.

When a business authorizes this connection:

- Customers communicate with the business through the business's connected WhatsApp number.
- Appvona processes relevant messages and messaging information to provide the requested integration and appointment services.
- The business remains responsible for its communications with its customers and for its use of customer information.
- WhatsApp and Meta may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Appvona does not represent that it owns or operates the WhatsApp platform.

## 6. How We Share Information

We may share information only when necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including:

- **Service providers:** Hosting, infrastructure, security, communications, and technical service providers that help us operate Appvona.
- **Connected platforms:** Third-party services that a business chooses to connect, such as WhatsApp.
- **Business customers:** Information about appointments and customers is made available to the relevant business and its authorized users.
- **Legal requirements:** Where disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority.
- **Business transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, resolve disputes, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

Businesses are responsible for determining appropriate retention periods for customer information they control. Where applicable, Appvona will handle deletion or return of business customer data in accordance with its agreements with the relevant business and applicable law.

Information may remain in secure backups for a limited period before being overwritten or deleted.

## 8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures may include access controls, authentication, encryption where appropriate, monitoring, and secure infrastructure.

However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Depending on the infrastructure and third-party services used, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where the individual or business is located.

Where required, we will take appropriate steps to ensure that international transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.
